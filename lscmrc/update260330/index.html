IMPORTANT - CHILDREN AT SHOWS

A show may seem an ideal event for children but shows can be long and children often get bored. From past experience we have to ask that children remain with you, under your control, at all times.

This is a direct quote from the club's handbook and so forms part of what is expected of all exhibitors at the shows held by the club.

To ensure a safe, respectful and enjoyable show day for everyone, we require all children to be supervised by a responsible adult at all times.

Recent events have shown that unsupervised behaviour can cause disruption, damage and safety risks. This is NOT ACCEPTABLE. Effective immediately:

All children must remain with their parent/carer whilst on the show premises.

Running, climbing on equipment or installations, noisy and/or disruptive behaviour will not be tolerated. Toys or other such items must not be left unattended on the hall floor.

If a child's behaviour becomes unsafe or inappropriate, we will ask the parent/carer to intervene immediately.

A repeat of the behaviour or similar will result in the family being asked to leave the hall immediately for the safety, enjoyment and comfort of others.

We appreciate your cooperation in helping us maintain a safe and respectful environment for all attendees.

REMINDER - Nominate two varieties (mice) for the Jubilee Cup 2026

Just to remind you that our Show Secretary, Terry Sales, is wanting your nominations for the (mice) Jubilee Cup for 2026. At the beginning of the year you nominate two varieties of your choice, and you will gain points for wins at members' shows with those varieties. The Jubilee Cup was introduced in 1965 and was a very popular competition in its early days with 11 exhibitors nominating varieties in one year. You cannot normally be retrospectively given points for the two varieties. We have made an exception this year. Even though the competition began in February we will accept your variety nominations provided they are received prior to the April Show, perhaps when making your entries. The results during the year for the Jubilee Cup have not appeared as it was necessary for it to be manually calculated. This has changed and they are now computer calculated as with all other points.

The 2025 results for the Jubilee Cup were as follows:

Jubilee Cup (Mice only)

E JUKES [Pearl, Silver Grey] 45
